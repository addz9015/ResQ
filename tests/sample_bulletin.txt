INDIA METEOROLOGICAL DEPARTMENT
TROPICAL CYCLONE ADVISORY BULLETIN NO. 15
BASED ON 0300 UTC OF 09.12.2022

SUB: Cyclonic Storm 'MANDOUS' (pronounced as Man-Dous) over southwest Bay of
Bengal, about 250 km east-southeast of Puducherry.

The Cyclonic Storm 'MANDOUS' over southwest Bay of Bengal moved
west-northwestwards with a speed of 12 kmph during the past 6 hours and lay
centered at 0300 UTC of 09th December 2022 near latitude 11.5 N and longitude
82.0 E, about 250 km east-southeast of Puducherry and 300 km south-southeast of
Chennai. The estimated central pressure is 998 hPa. Maximum sustained wind speed
of 65-75 kmph gusting to 85 kmph is prevailing around the system centre.

It is very likely to move west-northwestwards and cross north Tamil Nadu,
Puducherry and adjoining south Andhra Pradesh coasts between Puducherry and
Sriharikota around midnight of 9th December as a Cyclonic Storm.

Warnings:
(i) Heavy rainfall warning: Light to moderate rainfall at most places with heavy
    to very heavy falls at isolated places likely over Tamil Nadu.
(ii) Wind warning: Gale wind speed reaching 65-75 kmph gusting to 85 kmph likely
     along and off north Tamil Nadu and Puducherry coasts.
(iii) Sea condition: Rough to very rough.
(iv) Storm surge warning: Storm surge of about 0.5 m above astronomical tide
     likely to inundate low-lying areas.

An ORANGE message (be prepared) is in force for Chennai, Tiruvallur,
Chengalpattu, Kancheepuram and Villupuram districts of Tamil Nadu, valid till
1730 hours IST of 9th December 2022.
A YELLOW warning is in force for Cuddalore and Nagapattinam districts.

Fishermen are advised not to venture into the southwest and adjoining southeast
Bay of Bengal.
